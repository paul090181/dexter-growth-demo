Email connection guidance

Keep business and personal email separate.

GrowthWise recommends connecting a mailbox that is used only for your business. This helps protect personal privacy and gives AI-assisted customer communication a clean business-only workspace.

Before you connect a mailbox

If you authorize a personal mailbox, GrowthWise may be able to access personal messages under the email permissions you approve. You can choose to continue, but a dedicated business mailbox is strongly recommended.

Free separate mailbox

Create a separate Outlook.com address

A separate free Outlook.com account can be used only for business inquiries and keeps it separate from your personal Hotmail or Outlook inbox.

Open Microsoft Outlook

On Microsoft's site, choose Create free account. GrowthWise does not receive or store your Microsoft password.

Professional option

Use your own business domain

For a more professional address such as sales@yourbusiness.com, Microsoft 365 offers custom-domain business email. This is a paid Microsoft service and pricing can change.

View Microsoft business email options

What GrowthWise recommends

  • Use one mailbox primarily for customer inquiries and business communication.
  • Avoid connecting the same inbox you use for family, banking, healthcare, or other personal messages.
  • Start with read-only access while you review GrowthWise drafts.
  • Enable sending or automated replies only when you are comfortable with the workflow.
