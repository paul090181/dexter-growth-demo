Privacy Policy

How GrowthWise handles information while helping businesses manage products, marketing, customer leads, and connected services.

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GrowthWise ("GrowthWise," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when businesses and their customers interact with GrowthWise services.

Information we collect

Depending on the features a business connects or uses, GrowthWise may process:

  • Business account and contact information.
  • Product, inventory, order, pricing, sales, and marketing information supplied by the business or a connected service.
  • Customer inquiries and related conversation details received through Instagram and Facebook, email, websites, text messaging, or manual entry.
  • Photos, captions, product descriptions, and other content submitted for publishing or analysis.
  • Technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide requested features, organize business leads, prepare AI-assisted drafts and recommendations, connect authorized business services, publish content when a user requests it, troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and improve GrowthWise.

Instagram and Facebook data

When a business authorizes a Meta account, GrowthWise may receive account identifiers, authorized Page or Instagram Business Account information, messages, attachments, reactions, postbacks, read-status events, and related metadata made available through Meta's APIs. We use this information only to provide the connected inbox, customer-service, publishing, and reporting features requested by the business.

Disconnecting an account stops new authorized access. Information already received may remain only as long as reasonably necessary for the business workflow, security, legal obligations, backup integrity, or a valid deletion request.

AI-assisted features and Shadow Mode

GrowthWise may send relevant business information or customer inquiries to contracted AI service providers to create drafts, descriptions, summaries, or recommendations. During the current pilot, Shadow Mode evaluates suggested responses without automatically sending customer messages. A human remains responsible for reviewing and sending replies unless the business later enables a clearly disclosed automation feature.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us host, secure, analyze, or operate GrowthWise; with connected services when the business directs us to do so; or when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and data deletion

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with you, subject to applicable legal and operational exceptions. For a data deletion request, email engineerbit57@gmail.com with the subject “GrowthWise Data Deletion Request” and include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or conversation. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

Children's privacy

GrowthWise is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GrowthWise develops. The effective date above will change when a revised policy is posted.
